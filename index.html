Services for buildings, plants and scrap yards

One crew handles each job,
from the valuation to the last load of debris.

  • Building demolition

    Houses, offices, high-rise and tiled-roof buildings, sheds and factories, by machine or by hand.

  • Plant and machinery disposal

    Boilers, power plants, conveyors, EOT and gantry cranes, chillers, cooling towers, panels, cables and office IT.

  • Scrap buying and yard clearance

    Ferrous and non-ferrous metal, heavy melting scrap, structural steel, plastics and rejected pipe and sheet.

Demolish smart. Scrap right. Every job runs in three steps.

1

Value the scrap on site

We inspect the building or plant and price the scrap before any work starts.

2

Dismantle plant for more metal

We know where to cut and how to dismantle, so more metal comes back whole.

3

Clear the debris and scrap

Everything leaves on our lorries and the ground is handed back clear.

Safety, health and environment

Before any cut, we identify the hazards and assess the risk. Every worker wears PPE, gas cutting is controlled, a supervisor stays on site, and we follow your safety rules as well as the law.
